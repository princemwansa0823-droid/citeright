Common questions

Frequently asked

How does payment work?
50% is paid upfront when we confirm your order. Before releasing the final document we send you a preview screenshot so you can request any changes. Once satisfied, you pay the remaining 50%. We accept mobile money.
Is my information kept private?
Completely. We never share client details, topics, or work with anyone. Every enquiry and order is handled with complete discretion. Your identity and documents remain private at all times.
How long does it take?
Standard delivery is 48 hours from the time we confirm your order and receive the upfront payment. Urgent delivery (under 24 hours) is available for an additional K100–K150 depending on the complexity of the work.
Do you only serve UNZA students?
No — we serve students from any university or college. CiteRight is not affiliated with UNZA. We have completed work for students at UNZA, CBU, Mulungushi University, Cavendish, Zambia Open University, and international institutions.
Can I request revisions after delivery?
Yes. One round of revisions is included with every order. If you receive your work and your lecturer provides feedback, reach out to us — we will look at any adjustments needed at no additional charge within a reasonable scope.
